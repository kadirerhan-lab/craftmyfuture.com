CraftMyFuture — Privacy Policy

TASLAK / DRAFT — avukat incelemesi gerekli / requires review by a lawyer before publication. Effective date: [DD Month YYYY] · Version: 0.1 (draft)

In short, for kids

  • Right now CraftMyFuture keeps your game (your nickname, your character, your coins, jobs and skills) only on your own computer. We do not see it.
  • There are no ads, we do not track you, and nothing in the game costs real money.
  • Later you may be able to play with friends. A parent must say "yes" first, using the parent's e-mail — never yours.
  • Pick a nickname, never your real name. Never share your address, school, phone number or photos in the game.
  • If someone is mean or makes you feel uncomfortable: Block, Mute, Report, and tell a grown-up you trust.
  • Your parent can ask us to show or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

CraftMyFuture ("the game", "we", "us") is made by Kadir Erhan, an individual developer based in Türkiye. He is the data controller (KVKK) / controller (GDPR) / operator (COPPA) for personal data processed through the game.

Contact: kadirerhan84@gmail.com — please write "CraftMyFuture – Privacy" in the subject line. Postal address for formal applications: [address to be added before launch].

CraftMyFuture is a life-simulation game for children aged 10+. This policy is written mainly for parents, because many players are under 13.

2. The game today: single-player, data stays on the device

The current version is single-player and works offline. It saves your child's progress only in a file on the device where the game is installed. That file contains:

  • the in-game name/nickname your child chose,
  • character appearance (body, hair, outfit, colours),
  • in-game wallet and bank balance, savings, items bought,
  • jobs done, skills, Interest Paths and goals,
  • the last position in the game world (in-game location only — not the real location of the device).

We do not receive this file. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game's save data or uninstalling the game removes it.

The current game has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no real-money purchases. In-game coins are play money with no real value.

3. Planned online features (Phase 5) — not active yet

Optional online play with friends is planned. It will only be switched on after a parent gives verifiable consent; we will update this policy and ask for consent before anything goes live.

3.2 Communication is off until the parent turns it on

Communication is friends-only (no strangers, no public lobbies). The parent switches levels on one by one: emotes → preset quick chat → filtered text → voice. Text is filtered by Vivox Safe Text (bad words and personal information such as numbers, addresses and links are blocked). Every player can block, mute and report anyone.

3.3 Moderation, including an AI assistant

Reports first go to an AI moderation agent that sorts them by type and seriousness. It may only take automatic, reversible, low-risk actions (hide a message, mute for a session, a short chat cooldown, a friendly warning). It never bans anyone. People decide bans and all serious cases (for example bullying, grooming signs, self-harm signs, threats or sexual content). Urgent cases are e-mailed to the human moderator immediately; both sides' chat is frozen while it is reviewed. The AI receives only what the case needs — never e-mail addresses. Parents are informed of actions taken on their child's account.

Where the law requires it (for example apparent child sexual abuse material), a human moderator will report to the competent authorities and may preserve the related data for that purpose.

4. Legal bases (KVKK and GDPR)

  • Explicit consent of the parent/guardian (KVKK Art. 5(1) and 6; GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) with Art. 8) — online account for a child, communication features, voice, AI moderation of the child's messages.
  • Performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the game and the services the parent has turned on.
  • Legal obligation (KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç); GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) — legally required reports, responding to authorities.
  • Legitimate interest, balanced against the child's rights (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, preventing cheating and abuse, keeping chat safe.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time; this does not affect processing already done, but online features that depend on it will stop.

5. Children and parental consent

  • Türkiye (KVKK): for minors we rely on the consent of the parent or legal guardian.
  • EU / UK (GDPR): the age at which a child can consent alone varies by country between 13 and 16 (UK: 13). Below that age we require the consent of the holder of parental responsibility. To keep things simple and safe, every child account is created and approved with a parent's e-mail, whatever the age.
  • USA (COPPA): before collecting personal information from a child under 13 we give the parent notice and obtain verifiable parental consent. Parents can review their child's personal information, ask us to delete it, and refuse further collection or use at any time. We do not require a child to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to take part.

If we learn that we collected a child's personal data without the required consent, we delete it promptly.

7. Your rights

Under KVKK (Article 11) you can:

  1. learn whether personal data about you or your child is processed;
  2. request information about it;
  3. learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used in line with that purpose;
  4. know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred;
  5. ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected;
  6. ask for data to be erased or destroyed under KVKK Article 7;
  7. ask that corrections/erasures are notified to third parties who received the data;
  8. object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis;
  9. claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
