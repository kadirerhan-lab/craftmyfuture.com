In short, for kids

  • CraftMyFuture is a game for ages 10 and up. Have fun building your life!
  • To play online with friends, a parent must create or approve your account with their e-mail.
  • Use a nickname, never your real name.
  • Be kind. No bullying, no bad words, no tricks, and don't push anyone to give you gifts.
  • Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords or photos.
  • Game coins are only for the game. They are not real money and can't be swapped for real money or prizes.
  • If something feels wrong: Block, Mute, Report, and tell a grown-up.
  • Breaking the rules can mean a warning, a mute, or losing your account.

2. What CraftMyFuture is today and what is planned

Today CraftMyFuture is a single-player game. Progress is saved only on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no real-money purchases.

We plan to add optional online play with friends (accounts, friends, playing together, chat and voice). Sections 4–7 apply when those features are available. We will tell parents before they start.

3. Who can play (eligibility and parental consent)

  • The game is designed for players aged 10 and over.
  • Online features for children require verifiable parental consent. The account is created or approved using a parent's e-mail address; a child never gives their own e-mail.
  • The parent chooses which communication levels are switched on (emotes, quick chat, filtered text, voice). All of them are friends-only.
  • In the EU/UK, the age at which a child can consent alone varies between 13 and 16. To keep things simple, all child accounts are parent-approved.
  • A parent may withdraw consent at any time; online features then stop for that account.

By accepting these Terms, the parent confirms that they are the child's parent or legal guardian and have the right to give consent.

4. Accounts and unique names

  • Each player gets one account, linked to the approving parent's e-mail. Sign-in uses one-time codes; keep access to that e-mail safe.
  • The player chooses a unique nickname. It must not contain a real name, contact details, numbers that identify someone, links, bad words, or anything hateful or misleading (for example pretending to be staff or another person).
  • We may reject or reset a nickname that breaks these rules, and suggest a new one.
  • Accounts are personal: do not sell, share, lend or transfer them.
  • The parent is responsible for activity on the child's account as far as the law allows, and should tell us at kadirerhan84@gmail.com if they think the account has been misused.

5. Be kind and stay safe (acceptable behaviour)

When playing CraftMyFuture you must not:

  1. Share personal information — yours or anyone else's: real names, addresses, school, phone numbers, e-mail, social-media accounts, passwords, photos or meeting places.
  2. Bully, harass, threaten or exclude others, or use hateful, sexual, violent or bad language.
  3. Scam or pressure anyone: no tricks, no "give me your coins/items", no pressuring friends to send gifts, no promising things in return.
  4. Ask anyone to move the conversation outside the game or to meet in real life.
  5. Pretend to be someone else, including staff, a parent or another player.
  6. Cheat: use hacks, bots, modified clients, bugs (exploits) or anything that harms the game, other players or our servers. If you find a bug, please report it.
  7. Spam, advertise, or post links.
  8. Break the law or encourage others to do so.

Help keep everyone safe: use Block (they can no longer find or message you), Mute and Report. Children should always tell a trusted adult if something feels wrong.

6. Moderation and sanctions

  • Text chat is filtered automatically (Vivox Safe Text). Messages and reports are kept for 30 days for safety reviews, as explained in the Privacy Policy.
  • Reports are first sorted by an AI moderation assistant. It can only take small, reversible actions: hide a message, mute for the session, a short chat cooldown, or a friendly warning.
  • A human decides all account bans and serious cases (for example bullying, threats, grooming signs, self-harm signs, sexual content). Urgent cases are reviewed as a priority and chat may be paused for both sides while we look.
  • Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, we may: warn; remove content; mute or turn off chat; limit features; suspend the account temporarily; or close it permanently.
  • Where the law requires it, we will report to the authorities and may preserve related data.
  • Parents are informed of actions taken on their child's account. If you think we made a mistake, write to kadirerhan84@gmail.com; a human will review it.
