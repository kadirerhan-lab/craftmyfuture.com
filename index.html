Money basics, the fun way

Spend it, or save it?

Kids start with 200 coins. After a job they choose what to do with what they earned.

  • Treats at the Cafe give a short speed boost.
  • Caps, sunglasses and a propeller hat from the Market can be worn right away.
  • Save up 300 coins for a scooter and zoom around town.
  • Coins saved in the Bank grow a little after every job — saving pays off.

For parents

Safe by design

CraftMyFuture is made for children from the very first line of code. Here is what that means today — and what comes next.

No ads

There are no adverts in the game, ever.

No real-money purchases

Coins are play money. Nothing in the game costs real money and coins cannot be turned into real money.

Child-safe content rule

Every character, building and item is checked against a kid-appropriate content list before it gets into the game.

Privacy first

Today the game is single-player and saves progress only on your own device. We do not receive it.

Friends-only online Coming soon

Playing with friends is coming: friends-only (no strangers), a parent's e-mail and consent required, chat off until a parent turns it on, plus Block, Mute and Report.

Parent controls Coming soon

A parent page to approve the account, choose communication levels, and see or delete data.

FAQ

Questions from parents

What is CraftMyFuture?

A child-friendly life simulation. Players start at age 10 in the town of CraftVille, try jobs, earn coins, decide to spend or save, build skills and discover Interest Paths — areas they might enjoy, like Animal & Nature, Engineering or Entrepreneurship.

What age is it for?

CraftMyFuture is made for children aged 10 and up. Younger children can enjoy it with a grown-up.

Does it cost real money? Are there ads?

There are no ads and no real-money purchases. In-game coins are play money with no real value.

Can my child talk to strangers?

No. The game is single-player today. Online play is planned as friends-only, with a parent's consent, communication off by default and Block, Mute and Report on every social feature.

What data do you collect?

The game saves progress only on your device; we do not receive it. On this website, analytics cookies are used only if you click Accept in the cookie banner.

Where can we play?

In a web browser on a computer. A Windows download is coming soon; Steam, Xbox and mobile are planned later.

Is it educational?

Learning is built into play: earning and spending, saving with a growing bank balance, responsibility and patience on the job, and finding out what you enjoy — without lectures.
